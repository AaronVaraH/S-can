# Can Defect Classification and Inspection Catalog

## 1. Dents (Body and Rim Dents)
- **Description**: Mechanical deformations on the cylindrical can wall or rim caused by impact on conveyors, dead plates, or accumulation tables.
- **Classification**:
  - *Minor Body Dent*: Shallow depression (< 2mm depth) that does not crack lacquer or compromise seam.
  - *Major Body Dent*: Deep deformation (> 2mm depth) or sharp crease that may weaken the metal substrate or damage internal protective lining.
  - *Rim Dent (Critical)*: Any dent on or adjacent to the top or bottom double seam. Rim dents frequently compromise hermetic seal integrity and lead to microbiological contamination or vacuum loss.
- **Action**: Immediate line rejection for rim dents. Body dents above 2mm require FAIL.

## 2. Scratches and Abrasions
- **Description**: Linear surface marks caused by friction against metal guide rails or defective star wheels.
- **Classification**:
  - *Surface Scratch*: Friction mark on outer varnish with no exposed bare metal. Acceptable if isolated.
  - *Deep Gouge*: Penetration through external coating down to base tinplate or aluminum. High corrosion vulnerability.
- **Action**: Mark for REVIEW if isolated surface scratch. FAIL if gouge exposes base metal.

## 3. Double Seam Defects
- **Description**: Flaws in the hermetic seal connecting the can body and can end.
- **Key Seam Faults**:
  - *Droop*: A smooth projection of the double seam extending below the normal seam line, typically at the body seam crossover.
  - *Cut Seam*: The outer seam fractured along the top edge, exposing the metal core.
  - *False Seam*: Complete failure of the body hook and end hook to interlock.
- **Action**: CRITICAL FAIL. Never pass any seam anomaly. Actuate immediate reject and notify seamer mechanic.

## 4. Pinholes and Perforations
- **Description**: Microscopic through-wall holes caused by foreign object penetration, extreme corrosion, or raw material porosity.
- **Action**: CRITICAL FAIL. Immediate rejection.

## 5. Contamination and Foreign Matter
- **Description**: Grease, oil, syrup spills, chemical wash residues, or foreign particles adhering to the can exterior.
- **Action**: FAIL for food safety and packaging hygiene.
