# Industrial Double Seam Inspection Standards and Tolerances

## Overview of Double Seam Hermetic Integrity
A double seam is formed in two consecutive operational rolls:
1. **First Operation Roll**: Curls the end curl around the body flange to form interlocking body hook and end hook.
2. **Second Operation Roll**: Compresses and irons the interlocked layers flat against the can body, forming a gas-tight and liquid-tight seal with sealing compound.

## Dimensional Tolerances (Standard 2-Piece & 3-Piece Beverage/Food Cans)
- **Seam Thickness (ST)**: Nominal 1.10 mm to 1.35 mm (+/- 0.05 mm tolerance). Excess thickness indicates loose seaming or insufficient sealing compound compression.
- **Seam Length / Height (SL)**: Nominal 2.90 mm to 3.15 mm. Excess length indicates worn first-operation seaming rolls or excessive lifter pressure.
- **Body Hook (BH)**: Nominal 1.35 mm to 1.55 mm.
- **End Hook (EH)**: Nominal 1.35 mm to 1.55 mm.
- **Overlap (OL)**: Minimum required overlap is 1.00 mm (or > 55% of theoretical seam length).
- **Tightness Rating**: Minimum 75% wrinkle-free on the end hook radius.

## Visual Edge AI Inspection Guidelines
The edge camera inspects the perimeter of the double seam:
1. **Seam Waviness / Vee**: Visual sharp V-shaped projection at the base of the seam. Mark CRITICAL FAIL.
2. **Spinner / Slip**: Incomplete seam formation due to chuck slippage. Seam appears irregular with burnish marks.
3. **Compound Squeeze-out**: Gasket compound oozing out beneath seam edge.
4. **Sharp Seam / Cut Seam**: Sharp razor-like edge at the inside top of the seam.
