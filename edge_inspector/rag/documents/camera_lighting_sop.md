# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): Camera and Lighting Alignment for Metal Cans

## 1. Controlled Illumination Geometry
Reflective metal surfaces (aluminum and tinplate) produce intense specular reflection (hotspots and glare) under direct directional light. Glare blinds edge AI algorithms and washes out defect details.

### Recommended Illumination Configurations:
- **Diffuse Dome Illuminator (Cloudy Day Effect)**:
  - Best for general cylindrical can wall inspection and lithography defect detection.
  - Mount the dome light directly above the can station. Light bounces from the spherical interior, bathing the can in uniform, omnidirectional light with zero specular glare.
- **Darkfield Ring Light**:
  - Best for surface scratches, rim dents, and double seam vees.
  - Low-angle perimeter illumination (10 to 15 degrees from horizontal). Flat surfaces reflect light away from the camera, while scratches, dents, and seam burrs scatter light upward into the lens, appearing bright against a dark background.
- **Polarized Backlight / Coaxial Lighting**:
  - Used for top-down can end and pull-tab inspection.

## 2. Camera Positioning and Optical Setup
- **Working Distance (WD)**: Fixed at 250 mm to 350 mm from the conveyor center line.
- **Lens**: Low-distortion industrial C-mount lens (e.g. 12mm or 16mm focal length) with manual lockable aperture and focus rings.
- **Shutter Speed (Exposure Time)**: Set to <= 2.0 ms (2000 microseconds) for lines running up to 1200 cans per minute. Never use auto-exposure on high-speed conveyors.
- **Vibration Isolation**: Mount the camera and illuminator on independent rigid aluminum extrusion framing mechanically isolated from the conveyor drive motor.
